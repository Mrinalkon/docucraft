DocuCraft Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DocuCraft Pro ("the app") is built by an independent developer. This policy explains what information the app handles when you use it.

1. Documents and files you process

All PDFs, photos, and files you create, edit, sign, or convert are processed on your device only. Your documents are never uploaded to a developer server, and we do not read, store, or share them. Files are saved locally on your phone and can be deleted by you at any time (Profile → Delete account & data).

2. AI features (Google Gemini)

When you use the AI assistant, the text of your request (and, if you attach one, the content of that file or photo) is sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This content is processed under Google's own privacy terms (see Google Gemini API Privacy Notice). If you use your own Gemini API key, the request is tied to your personal Google account. Do not send confidential information to the AI that you are not comfortable sending to Google.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and usage data to personalize and measure ads. You can limit ad personalization in your device Settings → Google → Ads. See Google AdMob privacy policy for details.

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Subscriptions and credit purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only your purchase status (which product was bought) to unlock the corresponding features. Google handles all payment information; no card details ever reach the developer.

5. Data we store on your device

Your sign-in name, preferences, saved signatures, and document list are stored locally in your browser or app storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Delete account & data" removes this information permanently.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use.

7. Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, contact the developer from the app's Profile screen or the Google Play listing.
